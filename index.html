About
Strategic advisor at the sharp end of defense technology.

Marc C Lange advises investors, governments, and industry on unmanned systems, military autonomy, and defense procurement. Example: work on a counter-UAS program: testing kit in the field, red-teaming vendor claims, and advising procurement officials on what the technology actually does, versus what the press release says.

He is Strategic Program Advisor and Team Coach for AI, C2, and unmanned systems to the Federal German Breakthrough Innovation Agency (SPRIND), coaching 13 anti-drone startup teams in the Anti-Drone Response 2.0 competition. A member of the European Defence Agency's Autonomous Systems Community of Interest (EDA ASCI), he mentors and judges defense-tech suppliers (European Defence Fund, NATO).

"The variable that determines whether a capability changes the battlefield is not peak performance. It's cost-exchange ratio, innovation rate, and procurement-cycle speed."

Before defense: five years advising NASA Ames and ESA directorates on space technology investment; product leadership at Wayfair; rolled out Google's global startup program; CPO for Conversational AI at Deutsche Bahn, early AI startup founder. Governing Board member, International Space University and Roxolani Trust.

Advisory & Consulting
Where the engagement goes.

The work is analytical, not promotional. Clients are MoD procurement offices and strategic investors separating substance from vendor noise, and founders building in the defense-tech space. Entry point: the contact form at the end of this page.

Investment Due Diligence

Asking the hard questions: the pitch-to-reality gap. Red-teaming vendor capability claims. Cost-exchange modeling. Supply-chain fragility analysis two levels down. Production-rate verification against independently checkable delivery schedules.

Defense Procurement Strategy

Advising governments and supranational bodies on unmanned systems pre-procurement and acquisition programs, counter-UAS infrastructure protection, and the gap between announced budget and fielded capability.

Vendor & Technology Assessment

Field-testing kit. Pressure-testing autonomy and performance claims link by link through the kill chain. Delivered to five NATO and NATO-aligned governments.

Strategic Positioning, GTM & Fundraising

Defense-tech founders preparing for government contracts or investment rounds. Positioning for MoD and dual-use procurement. Go-to-market in the European and transatlantic defense market. Please contact Marc through your investor if you are interested in working with him.
